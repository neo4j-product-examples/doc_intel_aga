also the 60% owner of Meridian Holdings LLC (see that entity's KYC packet).

LLC's 20% stake, and indirectly through his majority ownership of Meridian
Holdings LLC's position in the Northgate/Cobalt ownership chain. Neither
path is disclosed on Anchor Point Partners LLC's own formation documents.
