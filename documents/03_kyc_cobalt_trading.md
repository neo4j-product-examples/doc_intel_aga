## 6. Due Diligence Notes

Note the reciprocal ownership: Cobalt Trading Co is 35%-owned by Northgate
Ventures LLC, and Cobalt Trading Co in turn holds a 30% stake in Meridian
Holdings LLC, which itself owns 40% of Northgate Ventures LLC. This forms a
closed ownership loop among the three entities (Meridian → Northgate →
Cobalt → Meridian). Looped ownership structures of this kind are not
inherently improper but do warrant closer review of the underlying economic
rationale, which has been requested from the client and is pending.

CFO Radu Costin holds no equity interest in Cobalt Trading Co or any
affiliated entity.
