**Publication:** Delaware Valley Business Journal
**Date:** 2023-08-22
**Headline:** State Examiners Flag Wilmington Suite as Registration Hub for Dozens of LLCs

State corporate-registry examiners have flagged a single office suite in
downtown Wilmington as the registered address for more than sixty limited
liability companies and corporations, raising questions about the use of
so-called "registered agent mills" in the state's business formation
system.

The suite, located at 1201 Harbor View Drive, Suite 400, serves as the
business address for Sterling Corporate Services LLC, a registered agent
firm that in turn lists the same suite as the principal address for a large
number of the entities it represents. Examiners reviewed a sample of filings
associated with the address, including those for Meridian Holdings LLC and
Cobalt Trading Co, two Wilmington-registered holding entities formed within
months of each other in 2019.

"There is nothing inherently improper about using a registered agent's
address as a company's mailing address — it's extremely common," said one
examiner quoted in the report. "What draws scrutiny is when a cluster of
entities registered at the same address also show overlapping ownership or
management, which can be a marker of layered corporate structures used to
obscure who ultimately controls a set of businesses."

The examiners' report did not allege wrongdoing by any specific company and
recommended continued monitoring rather than enforcement action. Sterling
Corporate Services LLC did not respond to a request for comment.
