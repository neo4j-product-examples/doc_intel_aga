**Publication:** International Finance Wire
**Date:** 2019-11-04
**Headline:** Former Ministry Advisor Named in Asset Disclosure Review

A former senior economic advisor to a European finance ministry, Dorian
Voss, was named this week in a government asset-disclosure review examining
whether a group of former political appointees used offshore holding
structures to manage personal investments while in public service.

Mr. Voss served as Senior Economic Advisor to the Ministry of Finance from
2014 to 2018, prior to returning to the private sector. The review, launched
by an independent government ethics office, examined disclosure filings from
several former officials but did not allege any specific violation by Mr.
Voss.

A spokesperson for Mr. Voss said he "fully cooperated with the review and
was cleared of any wrongdoing." Government records show the review was
formally closed in early 2020 with no charges filed against any of the
individuals examined, including Mr. Voss.
